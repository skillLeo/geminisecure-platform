# Privacy Policy

This policy covers the {{product}} app for residents and the {{product}} system your community uses. They are provided by **{{company}}** ("we", "us"), {{country}}.

{{product}} is a private platform for gated communities. It is **not an emergency service**. We do not sell personal information or show advertising. The app contains **no advertising or analytics trackers**.

## Who is responsible for your information

Your **community** (its committee or management company) decides who is on its register. {{company}} runs the {{product}} system on its behalf.

## What the app collects, and why

| Information | Why | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Your name, email address or phone number, and your home | To open your account and confirm with your estate office that you live there | Your estate office; {{company}} |
| Your household: members' names and how they are related to you, vehicle plates, and emergency contacts | So the gate knows who belongs to your home, and who to reach for you in an emergency | Your estate office; your community's security team at the gate (names and plates only) |
| Visitors you invite: their name, the reason for the visit, a vehicle plate, and the time they may enter | To let your visitor in at the gate | Your community's security team at the gate; your estate office |
| Photos you choose to add to a maintenance request or a report | To show the problem | Your estate office; the staff assigned to it |
| A profile photo, if you add one | To show on your profile | Only you, in the app |
| Your location, **only when you send an alert, or when you choose to add it to a report** | So your community's security team can find you | Your community's security team; your estate office |
| The make of your phone, an identifier for this installation of the app, and a notification token | To keep your account signed in on your phone only, and to send you notifications | {{company}} |
| Your notification choices and app settings | To respect them | {{company}} |

The app **shows** you your home's dues, charges, payments and receipts. It does not keep card details and does not process real card payments.

**Voting.** When your community holds an election in the app, your household's vote is stored with **nothing that links it to your household**. The community keeps a record that your home voted, and the day, but never how. Nobody can look up how a home voted: not you, not your estate office, and not {{company}}.

**Location.** The app never collects your location in the background. It asks for your location only while you are using the alert button or adding a location to a report, and only if you allow it. The alert works without it.

**Camera and photos.** The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo. Photos are saved again on your phone before they are sent, which removes hidden information such as where the photo was taken.

**Speaking a request.** If you choose to speak a report instead of typing it, your words are turned into text on your phone. No recording leaves it.

**App lock.** If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, the check is done by your phone. Your fingerprint or face never reaches the app or us.

## How long we keep information

- **Account details:** until the account is deleted.
- **Gate, visitor and alert records:** up to 7 years, as your community's security record. When an account is deleted, your identity is removed from them.
- **Your community's accounts** (charges, payments and receipts): for as long as accounting and tax law requires. They belong to the home, not to a person.
- **Requests to delete an account sent from our web page:** until they are dealt with, and no more than {{retention_days}} days after that.
- **Location:** kept only as part of the record it belongs to, such as an alert. It is never used for advertising or analytics.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account **in the app** (Profile → Delete my account) or **without the app** on our [account deletion page](/account/delete). Both explain, item by item, what is deleted at once and what must be kept.

## Who we share information with

- Your **estate office** and your community's **security team**, as set out above.
- **Service providers** that run the system for us: hosting, email delivery, and the notification services of Apple, Google and Expo, which deliver notifications to your phone. A notification says what kind of thing happened. It never contains a message's text, an amount of money, or a vote.
- **The authorities**, when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Security

Information travels encrypted. Your account's keys are kept in your phone's secure storage. Each community's records are kept separately, and staff access is limited by role and is logged.

## Your rights

Under Jamaica's Data Protection Act you may ask to see the personal information held about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted where the law does not require it to be kept. You can ask your estate office or us. You may also complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. A community may record a child as a member of a household. That is done by the adult who holds the account.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you.
